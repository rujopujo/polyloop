          E-Waste Regulations & ISO 11469 Reference

          India's E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022

          Under the revised EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) framework, electronic manufacturers and dismantlers must channel specific engineering plastics (ABS, PC, HIPS) to certified recyclers to prevent hazardous open burning or unscientific recycling.

          Brominated Flame Retardants (BFRs)

          High-temperature casings (monitors, printer chassis, TV back panels) often contain polybrominated diphenyl ethers (PBDEs). Incinerating them creates toxic dioxins and furans. They must be mechanically ground or pyrolyzed in controlled facilities.
