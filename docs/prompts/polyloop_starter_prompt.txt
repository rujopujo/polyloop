System Analysis of the Project Blueprint & Research Documents
The foundational architecture of PolyLoop (E-Waste Plastic Identification & Reuse System) addresses the circular economy gap where 20% to 30% of total e-waste mass consists of polymeric casings that are discarded due to polymer incompatibility and halogenated additive toxicity.
The system operates across three verified scientific domains:
1. Thermodynamic Miscibility & Degradation Mechanics: Macromolecular blending of post-consumer resins is constrained by positive Flory-Huggins interaction parameters ($\chi_{12} > 0$). Commingling polar Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS) with non-polar High-Impact Polystyrene (HIPS) causes severe micro-phase separation and over 70% loss in impact strength. Furthermore, Polycarbonate (PC) and PC-ABS blends are prone to hydrolytic chain scission if melt-extruded without precise moisture extraction below 0.02% to 0.03% (200 to 300 ppm).
2. Regulatory & Hazard Screening: Brominated Flame Retardants (BFRs) such as DecaBDE, OctaBDE, and TBBPA, frequently combined with antimony trioxide ($Sb_2O_3$), release toxic polybrominated dioxins and furans ($PBDD/Fs$) when re-melted above 210°C. International standards (EU RoHS 2011/65/EU, EU POPs Regulation 2019/1021, and WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU Annex VII) strictly cap PBDEs at 500–1,000 ppm. The system predicts BFR risk across appliance vintages (e.g., pre-2006 CRT units pose a 70%–90% risk, whereas modern post-2016 laptops pose $<10\%$ risk).
3. Three-Tiered Identification & Closed-Loop Upcycling: Replaces $30,000 NIR/XRF spectroscopy systems using a 100% free software stack:
   * Stage 1 (Object Detection): Ultralytics YOLOv8n classifies casing types (CRT housing, PC bezel, printer door).
   * Stage 2 (Embossed Stamp OCR): OpenCV image filtering (Grayscale $\rightarrow$ CLAHE $\rightarrow$ Bilateral Filter $\rightarrow$ Morphological Gradient $\rightarrow$ Otsu Binarization) enhances low-contrast reliefs for EasyOCR parsing of ISO 11469 / ISO 1043 codes (>ABS<, >PS-HI<, >PC+ABS<, >ABS-FR(40)<).
   * Stage 3 (Diagnostic Decision Wizard): Stratification fallback using specific gravity in tap water ($\rho = 1.00\text{ g/cm}^3$), 10% NaCl ($\rho = 1.07\text{ g/cm}^3$), and dense brine ($\rho = 1.15\text{ g/cm}^3$), combined with acetone and d-limonene spot solubility tests.
   * Stage 4 (Circular Upcycling & LCA Engine): Outputs exact 3-zone extruder temperatures (170°C–295°C), heated bed specs (85°C–130°C), and ISO 14040/14044 net carbon avoidance ($\Delta E_{\text{avoided}} = +3.02\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$ for ABS, $+6.02\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$ for PC) compiled into a downloadable ReportLab PDF Material Passport.

### A. Computer Vision & OCR Image Pipeline (ISO 11469 / ISO 1043)
1. **Component Classification:** Use Ultralytics YOLOv8n to identify e-waste components: `monitor_backing`, `pc_chassis`, `printer_panel`, `crt_housing`, `keyboard_casing`. 2. **OpenCV Contrast Enhancement for Low-Profile Mold Stamps:**
   - Input: Crop ROI from user bounding box or full image.
   - Step 1: Grayscale conversion: `cv2.cvtColor(roi, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)`
   - Step 2: Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE): `cv2.createCLAHE(clipLimit=2.5, tileGridSize=(8, 8))`
   - Step 3: Bilateral Denoising: `cv2.bilateralFilter(enhanced, d=9, sigmaColor=75, sigmaSpace=75)`
   - Step 4: Morphological Gradient: `cv2.morphologyEx(blur, cv2.MORPH_GRADIENT, cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_RECT, (3, 3)))`
   - Step 5: Otsu Binarization: `cv2.threshold(grad, 0, 255, cv2.THRESH_BINARY + cv2.THRESH_OTSU)`
3. **EasyOCR & Regex Token Extraction:**
   - Ingest the binarized image into EasyOCR (`reader.readtext`).
   - Extract standardized ISO tokens with regex: `r'>\s*([A-Z0-9|+|\-|\(|\)]+)\s*<'` and secondary fallback matching for unbracketed abbreviations: `ABS`, `HIPS`, `PS-HI`, `PC`, `PC+ABS`, `PC-ABS`, `PP`, `HDPE`, `PVC`.
   - Detect flame-retardant ISO 1043-4 codes: `FR(40)` (Halogenated + Sb2O3), `FR(14)` / `FR(52)` (Phosphorus/Red P).

$$\Delta E_{\text{avoided}} = M_{\text{recycled}} \cdot (EF_{\text{virgin}} - EF_{\text{recycling}}) + M_{\text{diverted}} \cdot EF_{\text{landfill\_credit}} - M_{\text{recycled}} \cdot EF_{\text{transp}}$$
Constants (kg CO₂e / kg):
- $EF_{\text{recycling}} = 0.75$
- $EF_{\text{landfill\_credit}} = 0.05$
- $EF_{\text{transp}} = 0.08$

- Virgin Resin Cradle-to-Gate ($EF_{\text{virgin}}$):

  - PC: 6.80 kg CO₂e/kg (Net Avoided: +6.02 kg CO₂e/kg; ~3.9 L crude oil)
  - PC-ABS: 5.10 kg CO₂e/kg (Net Avoided: +4.32 kg CO₂e/kg; ~2.9 L crude oil)
